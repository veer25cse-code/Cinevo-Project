About Cinevo BMU

Every story deserves a great screen.

Cinevo BMU is a student-built cinema discovery and ticket-booking website. It brings movie discovery, cinema selection, seat selection and booking confirmation together in one simple experience.

Discover movies

Explore now-showing and upcoming titles, search by name and browse genres.

Choose your experience

Compare cinemas, showtimes and seat categories before making a booking.

Book with confidence

Review your booking details and receive a confirmation page after submission.

This website is an academic demonstration project. Payments shown in the checkout are simulated and no real payment is processed.
